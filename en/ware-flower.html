Headquartered in Xi'an, Shaanxi, Zhengdaming Cultural Industry Group has branches in Ankang (Shaanxi) and Yangzhou (Jiangsu), with lacquer tree bases in Enshi, Chengkou, Shangluo and Zhuxi. Backed by in-depth research partnerships with the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Xi'an Raw Lacquer Research Institute, Tsinghua University and Northwest A&F University, its four brands — Zhengdaming Lacquer, Longtou Guoqi, Niuwang Guoqi and Suishiji — span materials, objects and lifestyle, with partners around the world.
