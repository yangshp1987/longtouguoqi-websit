Solution Overview

A gift worth giving clears three bars: the recipient wants to keep it, the giver can explain where it came from, and the organisation can account for it. Lacquerware clears all three — a recognised intangible-heritage craft behind it, a warm woody feel in the hand, and an inscription on the base that turns one gesture into something worth passing on.

Our corporate gifting service takes government bodies, cultural institutions and brands from budget tier and vessel selection through box design to bulk delivery. Every piece is made with natural lacquer from our own 5,000 mu of lacquer groves in the Qinba mountains, supervised by a provincial-level inheritor team, and carries an individual record: lacquer batch, artisan steps and finishing date, all traceable.

A gift with a provenance — and a future.

Headquartered in Xi'an, Shaanxi, Zhengdaming Cultural Industry Group has branches in Ankang (Shaanxi) and Yangzhou (Jiangsu), with lacquer tree bases in Enshi, Chengkou, Shangluo and Zhuxi. Backed by in-depth research partnerships with the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Xi'an Raw Lacquer Research Institute, Tsinghua University and Northwest A&F University, its four brands — Zhengdaming Lacquer, Longtou Guoqi, Niuwang Guoqi and Suishiji — span materials, objects and lifestyle, with partners around the world.
