Cultural Goods Solutions

Solution Overview

Lacquering techniques are inscribed on China's national representative list of intangible cultural heritage, but the word "heritage" has never meant protection alone — it means transformation. Many ICH centres, museums and cultural tourism groups hold rich cultural IP yet struggle to find a craft medium that can actually deliver, while many cultural product teams have ideas but cannot produce a qualified lacquerware sample. The ICH cultural products solution connects the chain: cultural IP + lacquer craft + modern product.

We work from three foundations: natural raw lacquer from our 5,000 mu of self-owned groves in the Qinba mountains; a team of artisans led by a provincial-level ICH inheritor of lacquer art; and an iF Design Award-winning design team. We provide end-to-end service from IP extraction, product planning and process sampling to batch production and traceable delivery. Every product carries its own file — lacquer batch, artisan procedures and completion date, all checkable.

Bringing Chinese lacquer out of the museum display case and into Eastern aesthetics you can take home.

Our Approach

A complete path from requirement to delivery, with standards, records and traceability at every step.

DiagnosisNeeds assessment: review IP assets, target audience and price band to define the product direction
PlanningProduct planning: the design team proposes a plan and sets the process route around the properties of lacquer
SamplingProcess sampling: artisans make samples by hand, refined repeatedly until both sides approve a sealed sample
ProductionBatch production: one record per piece, every procedure documented, lacquer batches traceable
Market launchDelivery and launch: packaging, certificates and traceability codes complete, ready for channel distribution
