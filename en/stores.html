Visitor Guide

Visitor Guide

🚗 Getting Here

By car: from Ankang city centre, follow National Highway G346 for about 40 minutes to Longtou Village; search "Longtou Village" in your navigation app.

Public transport: take a Pingli County coach from Ankang Railway Station or Ankang Intercity Bus Station, then change to a rural bus or taxi in the county town.

📅 Seasons to Visit

In tapping season (June–September) you can watch traditional tapping work — "a hundred li and a thousand cuts for one jin of lacquer". Spring and autumn suit hiking in the groves and inspecting lacquer seedlings.

🎫 Booking Notes

Individual visitors to the exhibition hall need no appointment. Group study tours, tapping experiences and base visits should be booked by phone three days in advance so that we can arrange guides and materials.

🧰 Notes for Your Visit

For the tapping experience, please wear long sleeves and trousers and non-slip shoes. Those allergic to raw lacquer are advised to observe rather than take part; children must be accompanied by a parent.

Book a Lacquer Journey

Call 130-7291-3676 and tell us your travel dates and group size; we will arrange a guided tour and hands-on experience for you.

Headquartered in Xi'an, Shaanxi, Zhengdaming Cultural Industry Group has branches in Ankang (Shaanxi) and Yangzhou (Jiangsu), with lacquer tree bases in Enshi, Chengkou, Shangluo and Zhuxi. Backed by in-depth research partnerships with the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Xi'an Raw Lacquer Research Institute, Tsinghua University and Northwest A&F University, its four brands — Zhengdaming Lacquer, Longtou Guoqi, Niuwang Guoqi and Suishiji — span materials, objects and lifestyle, with partners around the world.
