Brand Story

Brand Story

Follow the solar terms through the year, and bring Chinese lacquer back into everyday life.

'Suishiji' takes its name from 'suishi' — the wisdom by which the ancients arranged farming and daily life according to the solar terms. Growth in spring, maturing in summer, harvest in autumn, storage in winter: time flows through the twenty-four marks, and also through the waiting of lacquer curing.

The making of a lacquer object is much like the solar terms: one coat of lacquer, several days of curing in a humid chamber — it cannot be hurried, nor delayed. Suishiji turns this Eastern philosophy of 'living with time' into objects that can be worn, handled and used every day.

From a pair of Chinese lacquer earrings to a lacquer fan, from solar-term gift boxes to custom gifting, Suishiji engages a material of a thousand years with youthful design, releasing Chinese lacquer from the museum label and returning it to people's wrists, desks and daily lives.

The solar terms do not age; they simply move into life in a new way.

Brand Positioning

Brand Positioning

The solar terms as the calendar, Chinese lacquer as the language.

A cultural and creative brand of Eastern lifestyle aesthetics

Suishiji is positioned as a cultural and creative brand of Eastern lifestyle aesthetics, using the 24 solar terms as its product calendar and Chinese lacquer material as its intangible cultural heritage gene, serving two customer groups: young consumers and custom gifting clients.

The brand translates Chinese lacquer material and contemporary design into one another: three product directions — jewelry, lacquer fans and solar-term cultural creations — let traditional craft enter everyday wear and lifestyle scenarios in a youthful form.

Brand Vision

Brand Vision

Let a lacquer of a thousand years become part of this generation's everyday life.

Tradition is not retro; it is re-translated once by every era.

Suishiji's vision is to complete Chinese lacquer's turn from 'cultural heritage' to 'lifestyle': when young people think of a piece of lacquerware as the solar terms change, intangible cultural heritage truly lives in the present.
