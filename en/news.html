Media Coverage

Media Coverage

Mainstream media including People's Daily, Xinhua News Agency and CCTV have continued to follow the inheritance and innovation of Niuwang raw lacquer and the Chinese lacquer industry.

People's Daily · People's Daily Online

《Contributing the Strength of Youth to Promoting Fine Traditional Culture》

The 'Youth' column of People's Daily reported on Yuan Duanjiao's return to her hometown to start a business and her practical work of driving rural revitalization through the Chinese lacquer industry.

China Youth Net

《The Lacquer Fairy's Lacquer-Bright Dream》

The report focuses on the Chinese lacquer art journey and the original aspiration of Yuan Duanjiao, a provincial-level intangible cultural heritage inheritor; fellow villagers affectionately call her the 'Lacquer Fairy'.

Sina Finance

《A Post-80s Master's Graduate Returns Home to Study His Hometown's Native Lacquer》

It tells the story of highly educated talent returning home to deepen their work in the Chinese lacquer industry and driving the modernization of traditional crafts.

Xinhua Shaanxi Channel

《Shaanxi Pingli: Traditional Lacquer Art Takes on New Splendor》

Xinhua reported that Yuan Duanjiao created a 'water shadow painting' using lacquer as ink at the 'Silk Road at the Fingertips — China-Uzbekistan Women's Innovation and Entrepreneurship Cooperation Forum'.

Xinhua Shaanxi Channel

《China in Focus丨Innovation in Intangible Cultural Heritage Blooms in Lacquer》

Xinhua published a photo report on Yuan Duanjiao discussing Chinese lacquer coating techniques with her father Yuan Huizhi, recording the continuation of this craft in the hands of a new generation.

China Central Television, The Path to Prosperity

CCTV Feature Program 《A Hundred Li and a Thousand Cuts for One Jin of Lacquer: Unveiling the Lacquer Tappers of the Deep Mountains》

The CCTV finance channel program The Path to Prosperity visited Baxian Town in Pingli, with a feature report on lacquer tappers in the deep mountains and the Niuwang raw lacquer intangible cultural heritage technique.

People's Daily Online Shaanxi Channel

《The Beauty of Chinese Lacquer: Lustrous and Radiant》

People's Daily Online visited the Niuwang Raw Lacquer Intangible Cultural Heritage Exhibition Hall, recording how lacquerware is returning to everyday life in the form of vases, bracelets, lacquer boxes and tea sets.

Shaanxi Daily · Sanqin Online

《Yuan Duanjiao: Safeguarding a Millennia-Old Craft with Dedication》

Shaanxi Daily published a full-page report on how Yuan Duanjiao safeguards the Niuwang Raw Lacquer Painting Technique with a craftsman's dedication, bringing the millennia-old lacquer art into full bloom.

CRI Shaanxi Channel

《Yuan Duanjiao, Representative Inheritor of Intangible Cultural Heritage: A Life Aglow with Lacquer》

CRI reported that Yuan Duanjiao studied under renowned lacquer artists and has mastered a range of lacquering techniques, and that she received the Excellence Award of the 'Xinchuan Award'.

Shaanxi Party Building Website

《The Colors of Lacquer》

Shaanxi Party Building Website published a feature profile recording how Yuan Duanjiao revived her ancestors' old 'Zhengdaming' firm from the late Qing period and used e-commerce to bring the Niuwang Guoqi lacquer craft out of the remote mountains.

Ankang Municipal People's Government

Niuwang Guoqi Appears at the 20th Xi'an International Tea Expo

Pingli County organized leading local enterprises to take part in the exhibition. Shaanxi Longtou Guoqi Cultural Industry Co., Ltd. set up a custom-built booth in the dedicated raw lacquer craft zone, bringing lacquerware such as teacups and tea scoops designed for tea-drinking occasions.

Belt and Road Converged Media Communication Campaign

Belt and Road Converged Media Delegation Visits Pingli to Experience the Intangible Cultural Heritage Floating Lacquer Technique

The press delegation of the 'Keeping the World Moving' converged media campaign visited the Chinese Lacquer Museum in Longtou Village to see the transformation of this mountain village firsthand.
