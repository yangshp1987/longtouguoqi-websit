Niuwang Guoqi Appears at the 20th Xi'an International Tea Expo

2026-06 · Ankang Municipal People's Government

From June 5 to 8, the 20th Xi'an International Tea Expo was held at the Xi'an International Convention and Exhibition Center. Pingli's selenium-rich tea, Niuwang Guoqi and other signature products were presented together, including handmade raw lacquer craft ornaments for the Qin-Han ancient tea.

Key points of the report

  • Pingli County gave a special promotion titled Tea Fragrance Nourishes a Millennium, Chinese Lacquer Shines over Qinba, highlighting five representative products: Nüwa tea, selenium-rich matcha, gynostemma, Qin-Han ancient tea and Niuwang Guoqi;
  • Pingli County organized five high-quality local enterprises to set up custom-built booths in the dedicated zones for selenium-rich tea and raw lacquer craftsmanship;
  • Shaanxi Longtou Guoqi's participation focused on the combination of tea and lacquer, bringing a range of lacquerware suited to tea-drinking occasions, such as teacups and tea scoops;
  • Visitors at the expo commented that Pingli lacquerware is made with natural Chinese lacquer and mineral pigments, making it environmentally friendly as well as both practical and ornamental.

Direct quotes

'The Xi'an Tea Expo has provided an excellent platform for display and exchange, letting more people learn about Pingli raw lacquer and Pingli lacquerware, and effectively raising the visibility and influence of Niuwang Guoqi.' — Yuan Duanjiao, head of Shaanxi Longtou Guoqi Cultural Industry Co., Ltd.

Headquartered in Xi'an, Shaanxi, Zhengdaming Cultural Industry Group has branches in Ankang (Shaanxi) and Yangzhou (Jiangsu), with lacquer tree bases in Enshi, Chengkou, Shangluo and Zhuxi. Backed by in-depth research partnerships with the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Xi'an Raw Lacquer Research Institute, Tsinghua University and Northwest A&F University, its four brands — Zhengdaming Lacquer, Longtou Guoqi, Niuwang Guoqi and Suishiji — span materials, objects and lifestyle, with partners around the world.
