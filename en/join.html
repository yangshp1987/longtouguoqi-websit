Talent Philosophy

"Integrity in the heart, honesty in the vessel." We are looking for people willing to settle down and do one thing well.

People who keep the craft, and people who get things done

Zhengdaming grew out of an old trading house founded in 1925, and today is an industry group covering lacquer tree planting, raw lacquer collection, refining and processing, lacquerware making and cultural product development. The lacquer trade cannot be hurried — a lacquer tree takes five years before it can be tapped, and one piece of lacquerware needs eight coats of lacquer and months of curing in a humid chamber. We believe people are the same: slower is often faster.

Here you touch the full industry chain from lacquer grove to exhibition hall, learn traditional techniques from a provincial-level representative inheritor of intangible cultural heritage, and take part in designing products for contemporary life. We respect the craft, and we respect everyone who makes the craft their career.

If "integrity in the heart, honesty in the vessel" speaks to you, we welcome you to join us.

Headquartered in Xi'an, Shaanxi, Zhengdaming Cultural Industry Group has branches in Ankang (Shaanxi) and Yangzhou (Jiangsu), with lacquer tree bases in Enshi, Chengkou, Shangluo and Zhuxi. Backed by in-depth research partnerships with the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Xi'an Raw Lacquer Research Institute, Tsinghua University and Northwest A&F University, its four brands — Zhengdaming Lacquer, Longtou Guoqi, Niuwang Guoqi and Suishiji — span materials, objects and lifestyle, with partners around the world.
