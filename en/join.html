Talent Philosophy

"Integrity in the heart, honesty in the vessel." We are looking for people willing to settle down and do one thing well.

People who keep the craft, and people who get things done

Zhengdaming grew out of an old trading house founded in 1925, and today is an industry group covering lacquer tree planting, raw lacquer collection, refining and processing, lacquerware making and cultural product development. The lacquer trade cannot be hurried — a lacquer tree takes five years before it can be tapped, and one piece of lacquerware needs eight coats and months of curing. We believe people are the same: slower is often faster.

Here you touch the full industry chain from lacquer grove to exhibition hall, learn traditional techniques from a provincial-level ICH inheritor, and take part in designing products for contemporary life. We respect the craft, and we respect everyone who makes the craft their career.

If "integrity in the heart, honesty in the vessel" speaks to you, we welcome you to join us.

Open Positions

Open Positions

These positions are open on an ongoing basis; please refer to the latest postings for details. CVs are welcome at any time.

Lacquer Technician / Apprentice

Lacquering, gold tracery and inlay. No prior experience required — the company provides systematic training under ICH inheritors.

Lacquerware Product Design

Responsible for design and development of lacquerware and cultural creative products; a background in industrial design or arts and crafts preferred.

E-commerce Operations / Livestream

Responsible for store operations and livestream selling on Taobao, Douyin and other platforms; experience in the lacquer or art collectibles category preferred.

International Trade Specialist

Responsible for developing overseas markets for raw lacquer and lacquerware; English or Japanese communication skills preferred.

Lacquer Grove Base Management

Responsible for day-to-day care of the lacquer groves and production management during tapping season; farming experience preferred; based at the Pingli site.

Study Tour Instructor / Exhibition Hall Host

Responsible for guiding at the ICH exhibition hall and delivering lacquer study courses; standard Mandarin and a warm manner required.
