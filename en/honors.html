Honors & Awards

Honors and Qualifications at a Glance

From the Qinba lacquer forests to the international stage, every recognition and award marks a stretch of the road this craft has traveled.

Techniques and Intangible Cultural Heritage Recognition

Heritage & Qualification

2022

Niuwang Raw Lacquer Lacquering Techniques · The Seventh Batch of the Representative List of Intangible Cultural Heritage of Shaanxi Province

Covering three aspects — lacquer tapping, lacquer making and lacquer coating — it forms a complete technical system from tapping and refining to lacquering, with Yuan Duanjiao as the inheritor of this line of transmission.

2023

Shaanxi Provincial Intangible Cultural Heritage Workshop

The company was named a 'Shaanxi Provincial Intangible Cultural Heritage Workshop', and designated as the Ankang Niuwang Guoqi Inheritor Training Base and an ICH poverty-alleviation employment workshop.

2020

Drafter of Lacquer Tree Industry Standards

Participated in drafting two industry standards: 'Technical Regulations for Lacquer Tree Afforestation' GH/T 1299-2020 and 'Technical Specifications for Pest and Disease Control of Lacquer Trees' GH/T 1300-2020.

Science and Technology Innovation Demonstration Base of the Xi'an Raw Lacquer Coatings Research Institute, All China Federation of Supply and Marketing Cooperatives

Recognized as a science and technology innovation demonstration base for lacquer tree breeding and raw lacquer refining, with long-term industry-academia-research cooperation with Northwest A&F University and the Xi'an Raw Lacquer Research Institute.

School-Enterprise Cooperation and Internship & Training Demonstration Base

Named a school-enterprise cooperation / internship and training demonstration base by the College of Fine Arts Education of the Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts, the Art Design Department of Hubei Industrial Polytechnic, Shanghai Art & Design Academy and the School of Handicraft Arts of Sichuan Fine Arts Institute.

Municipal-level Forestry Industry Demonstration Base

Cultivated as a municipal-level forestry industry demonstration base, and pioneered a 'company + cooperative + farmer' interest linkage mechanism.

Works and Design Awards

Awards & Design

2025

German iF Design Award · Modular Custom Chinese Lacquer Eyewear

Developed in collaboration with a teacher-student team from Xi'an Jiaotong University, it stood out among more than ten thousand entries from around the world, bringing Chinese lacquer art onto the international design stage.

2025

'Baihe New Talent Award', China Arts and Crafts Expo · 'Yunjian Jianlongxiang'

Made with the bodiless (coreless) lacquer technique and an original 'layered incised gold' method: black lacquer as the ground, incised and traced with gold, completed through 72 procedures.

2025

First Prize, 3rd China Lacquer Art Expo · 'Five Oxen Scroll Jar'

A large-scale lacquer art work that carries ancient methods while harboring new ideas; works from the same period, including the carved lacquer vase 'Songhe Yannian' (Pine and Crane) and the 'Nüwa Mending the Sky' root-carving lacquerware, also won recognition within the industry.

2025

Shaanxi 'Chuanshi Zhenpin' Title · 'Menghuan'

The work 'Menghuan' was awarded the title of Shaanxi 'Chuanshi Zhenpin' (Treasured Masterpiece).

2021

Excellence Award, China Intangible Cultural Heritage Expo · 'Biyue Xiuhua'

The lacquer art work 'Biyue Xiuhua' received an Excellence Award at the China Intangible Cultural Heritage Expo.

2022

Excellence Award, China ICH Inheritor Xinchuan Award · 'Biyue Xiuhua' Series

Received an Excellence Award at the Xinchuan Award Traditional Crafts Exhibition held by the Department of Culture and Tourism of Zhejiang Province and the Hangzhou Municipal People's Government.

2019

First Prize, Shaanxi Provincial Arts and Crafts Protection Project

Works including 'Spring', 'Lan' and 'Nüwa Mending the Sky' won awards and were collected by the Shanghai Minhang Lacquerware Museum and the Museum of Northwest A&F University; the gold beetle lacquerware production project received the same award.

2020

First Prize, 'Lishan Cup' Lacquer Art Cultural Creative Design Competition

The lacquer cultural creative work won first prize at the 'Lishan Cup' Lacquer Art Cultural Creative Design Competition.

Personal Honors

Personal Honors

Arts and Crafts Master of Shaanxi Province

Yuan Duanjiao was named an Arts and Crafts Master of Shaanxi Province; she studied under the national lacquer art masters and China Arts and Crafts Masters Du Zhongxun and Chen Tengan.

Provincial-level Representative Inheritor of Intangible Cultural Heritage

As the provincial-level representative inheritor of the Niuwang Raw Lacquer Lacquering Techniques, she has mastered a range of lacquering techniques including biantu (varied coating), gold tracery, pattern raising, piled lacquer and mother-of-pearl inlay.

2025

China Rural Crafts Master / Shaanxi Provincial Rural Crafts Master

Named a China Rural Crafts Master and a Shaanxi Provincial Rural Crafts Master, and selected for the first 'Local Culture Talents' list of Ankang.

'Jinzhou Craftsman'

Named a 'Jinzhou Craftsman', and affectionately called the 'Lacquer Fairy' by fellow villagers.

Industrial and Social Impact

Industrial & Social Impact

2025

Processing over 200 tons of raw lacquer annually, with an output value of more than RMB 50 million

Processing more than 200 tons of raw lacquer a year and achieving an output value of over RMB 50 million, directly raising the income of 300 lacquer farmer households across the county on a stable basis.

The 'Lacquer Farmers' School' has trained more than 300 local technical workers

Established an inheritor training base, an ICH workshop and a 'Lacquer Farmers' School', systematically teaching the complete set of techniques through offline classes, online livestreams and instructional videos.

Intelligent upgrading: lacquer liquid transparency 72% → 89%

In 2023, intelligent mixing and constant-temperature equipment was introduced, together with power grid upgrades in the production area; the transparency of the lacquer liquid rose from 72% under the traditional process to 89%, and the product scrap rate fell to 0.05%.

Over 800 mu of transferred lacquer gardens and more than 1,300 mu of self-owned lacquer gardens

Providing lacquer saplings and technical training in a unified way and purchasing uniformly during the tapping season, the company binds more than 600 lacquer farmers through three channels: employment in the park, market purchase and cooperative profit sharing.

View media coverage Explore the brand portfolio

Headquartered in Xi'an, Shaanxi, Zhengdaming Cultural Industry Group has branches in Ankang (Shaanxi) and Yangzhou (Jiangsu), with lacquer tree bases in Enshi, Chengkou, Shangluo and Zhuxi. Backed by in-depth research partnerships with the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Xi'an Raw Lacquer Research Institute, Tsinghua University and Northwest A&F University, its four brands — Zhengdaming Lacquer, Longtou Guoqi, Niuwang Guoqi and Suishiji — span materials, objects and lifestyle, with partners around the world.
