Solution Overview

Pingli is designated China’s “Home of the Lacquer Tree”, and is the Jinzhou of the Ming-dynasty Compendium of Materia Medica, which noted that “the lacquer of Jinzhou is best, hence it is called Jin lacquer”. The Niuwang lacquer produced here has high urushiol content and reliable drying behaviour — the material of choice for heritage restoration, wood finishing and industrial anti-corrosion work.

We manage more than 5,000 mu of lacquer groves in the Qinba mountains and control the whole chain, from tapping and shade-drying to filtering and inspection. Wholesale customers receive a third-party test report and a retained sample for every batch. From kilo-scale trial samples to tonne-scale annual contracts, supply holds.

A hundred li and a thousand cuts for one jin of lacquer — and every jin has a traceable origin.

Headquartered in Xi'an, Shaanxi, Zhengdaming Cultural Industry Group has branches in Ankang (Shaanxi) and Yangzhou (Jiangsu), with lacquer tree bases in Enshi, Chengkou, Shangluo and Zhuxi. Backed by in-depth research partnerships with the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Xi'an Raw Lacquer Research Institute, Tsinghua University and Northwest A&F University, its four brands — Zhengdaming Lacquer, Longtou Guoqi, Niuwang Guoqi and Suishiji — span materials, objects and lifestyle, with partners around the world.
