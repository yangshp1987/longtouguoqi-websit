Brand Story

Brand Story

The memory of a time-honored brand, the transmission of a craft, an Eastern etiquette.

The name Niuwang Guoqi holds the memory of a time-honored brand. The old Niuwang Raw Lacquer trading house once stood among the lacquer market streets of the Qinba mountains — level scales, full measures, genuine lacquer: the rules kept by generations of lacquer merchants, and the rules Niuwang Guoqi still keeps today.

Today, this craft is listed under the name 'Niuwang Raw Lacquer Lacquering Techniques' in the provincial intangible cultural heritage list. Building on this foundation, Niuwang Guoqi works with the Niuwang Raw Lacquer Farmers Professional Cooperative and the Niuwang Raw Lacquer Research Institute to bring planting, technique and R&D together as one.

In the Niuwang Raw Lacquer ICH Exhibition Hall, tapping knives, lacquer sieves and lacquering benches are open to the public — study tour courses let visitors experience the whole process from lacquer tree to finished object with their own hands. Chinese lacquer culture is no longer an exhibit behind glass, but living memory that can be seen, learned and passed on by hand.

Niuwang Guoqi focuses on official gifting, cultural collections, ICH study tours and lacquer tapping experiences — so that Chinese lacquer culture is more than display, but a cause the next generation can take over.

Transmission means letting the next pair of hands take over the warmth of the previous pair.

Brand Positioning

Brand Positioning

Rooted in provincial-level ICH techniques, carrying etiquette through objects.

Intangible cultural heritage brand

Niuwang Guoqi is positioned as an intangible cultural heritage brand, rooted in the provincial-level transmission of lacquering techniques and carrying the memory of a time-honored brand, focusing on four scenarios: official gifting, cultural collections, ICH study tours and lacquer tapping experiences.

The Niuwang Raw Lacquer Farmers Professional Cooperative and the Niuwang Raw Lacquer Research Institute provide the brand with planting, technique and R&D support — from the lacquer in the forests to the classes in the exhibition hall, Niuwang Guoqi presents a complete intangible cultural heritage chain to the public.

Brand Vision

Brand Vision

Let intangible cultural heritage live in everyday life, not remain in a display case.

The surest protection for intangible cultural heritage is to make it needed by life again.

Niuwang Guoqi's vision is to move intangible cultural heritage from 'being protected' to 'being needed': giving the craft inheritors, giving lacquer farmers an income, and giving the public an entry point to touch Chinese lacquer, forming a self-sustaining living transmission.

Passing on the craftBringing ICH techniques into curricula and study tours, letting young people touch Chinese lacquer with their own hands

Enriching peopleThe cooperative connects lacquer farmers, so that those who keep the craft benefit first
