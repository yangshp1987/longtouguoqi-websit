Industrial Application Solutions

Solution Overview

Raw lacquer is nature's gift to industry: once urushiol forms a film it resists acid, alkali, salt spray and high temperature, and outperforms ordinary synthetic coatings in adhesion and ageing behaviour. From the lacquered coffin at Mawangdui to the hulls of modern ships, anti-corrosion capability proven over eight thousand years is being rediscovered in industrial settings.

But industrial lacquer is not as simple as buying a drum of raw lacquer: different working conditions call for different urushiol content and drying curves, and different substrates call for different surface preparation. Our industrial application solution uses a four-step method — selection, testing, adaptation, supply — to turn the properties of natural raw lacquer into a coating solution that runs reliably on the customer's production line.
