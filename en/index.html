Group Profile

Group Profile

Building on natural raw lacquer resources, Zhengdaming Cultural Industry Group has constructed a complete industry chain of 'lacquer tree planting — raw lacquer tapping — refining — ICH objects — lacquer jewelry', and is home to a representative inheritor of Shaanxi provincial intangible cultural heritage.

Integrity in the heart · clarity in the vessel — a Chinese lacquer cultural industry group with a hundred years of history

Zhengdaming Cultural Industry Group (Zhengdaming Cultural Industry Group) has been devoted to natural Chinese lacquer for a century. Rooted in the natural raw lacquer resources of the core production area of Niuwanggou in Pingli, Ankang, Shaanxi, it covers the entire industry chain of 'lacquer tree planting → raw lacquer tapping → refining → lacquerware production → cultural communication'. It operates five business entities: Shaanxi Zhengdaming Cultural Industry Co., Ltd., Shaanxi Longtou Guoqi Cultural Industry, Xi'an Qiqi Hantang Cultural Industry, the Niuwang Raw Lacquer Farmers Professional Cooperative and the Niuwang Raw Lacquer Research Institute.

Headquartered in Xi'an, Shaanxi Province, the group has branches in Ankang, Shaanxi and Yangzhou, Jiangsu, and lacquer tree bases in Enshi, Chengkou, Shangluo and Zhuxi. Drawing on in-depth research collaboration with the Chinese Academy of Sciences, the Xi'an Raw Lacquer Research Institute, Tsinghua University and Northwest A&F University, its four brands — Zhengdaming Lacquer, Longtou Guoqi, Niuwang Guoqi and Suishiji — cover the full 'materials — objects — life' spectrum, with partners in markets around the world.

Timeline

Milestones

A century of lacquer: from the trading house years to the brand portfolio, every step Zhengdaming has taken.

1925
The Zhengdaming trading house was founded in the lacquer country of the Qinba mountains, specializing in the purchase and transport of raw lacquer.
1928
Established joint-operation partnerships with trading houses including Zhou Chengtai, shipping lacquer from Baijiawan via the Han River to Hankou for distribution.
1931
Opened channels in Shanghai; raw lacquer was transshipped through the Port of Shanghai and sold as far as Japan.
1934
Developed refined colored lacquer, completing the key leap from raw material trading to deep processing of lacquer products.
1938
With shipping on the Yangtze blocked and exports suspended, the company fell back on its Qinba raw lacquer base, 'taking the mountains as its warehouse' to protect lacquer farmers and lacquer trees.
1942
Raw lacquer was designated a wartime material and supplied for military needs, preserving the network of lacquer farmers and the craft of lacquer blending.
1956
Under public-private joint operation, business names were merged; Pingli County set up a raw lacquer research institute and the raw lacquer business was consolidated into foreign trade companies and native produce companies.
1985
Raw lacquer exports fully resumed; Pingli, as the core production area of the 'China Lacquer Capital', re-entered the international market, and the ancient lacquering techniques handed down from Zhengdaming continued to be practiced among the people.
2014
Established Shaanxi Longtou Guoqi Cultural Industry Co., Ltd., carrying forward the century-old lacquer lineage of Zhengdaming.
2017
Established the Niuwang Raw Lacquer lacquer tree planting base, covering an area of about 5,000 mu.
2018
Established the Niuwang Raw Lacquer Lacquering Techniques intangible cultural heritage inheritance base, using intangible cultural heritage to drive poverty alleviation through industry.
2019
Founded the Pingli Niuwang Raw Lacquer Research Institute.
2020
Formulated the lacquer tree industry standards 'Technical Regulations for Lacquer Tree Afforestation' (GH/T 1299-2020) and 'Technical Specifications for Pest and Disease Control of Lacquer Trees' (GH/T 1300-2020).
2022
Niuwang Raw Lacquer Lacquering Techniques was included in the seventh batch of the Representative List of Intangible Cultural Heritage of Shaanxi Province.
2022
Xi'an Qiqi Hantang Cultural Industry Co., Ltd. was established.
2023
Recognized as a 'Shaanxi Provincial Intangible Cultural Heritage Workshop'.
2025
The work 'Menghuan' (Dreamscape) was awarded the title of Shaanxi 'Chuanshi Zhenpin' (Treasured Masterpiece); the modular eyewear work received the German iF Design Award; 'Yunjian Jianlongxiang' received the New Talent Award of the China Arts and Crafts Baihe Award; and Yuan Duanjiao was named a China Rural Crafts Master.

Historic Building Solutions

Providing complete solutions for the surface coating of historic buildings in pure Chinese lacquer techniques — ground layer materials, ash application with hemp backing, surface lacquering and other traditional processes, restoring the effect of traditional Chinese lacquer architecture.

Guqin Solutions
